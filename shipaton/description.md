# Lingua — Shipaton 2026 submission (draft)

## Tagline
Make your own language — draw its letters, build its words, and write in it with your own keyboard.

## What it is
Lingua is a conlang (constructed language) studio for iPhone. You draw your own alphabet with your finger, and Lingua turns it into a real font on the device. You then build the language around it — words, sounds, grammar — and write in it anywhere inside the app with a system keyboard made from the letters you drew.

## What you can do
- **Draw your alphabet.** Every language starts with its own a–z, ! and ? and a digit for every number, ready to draw on. Strokes, fills and rounding are turned into a font on the phone, so what you drew is what you type.
- **Type in your own letters.** Lingua ships an iOS keyboard extension. The free plan gives you a QWERTY where every key is a letter you drew; paid plans let you design your own layouts.
- **Build the language.** A dictionary with meanings, readings and example sentences; a phonology chosen from real IPA; a grammar engine that writes and reads sentences in your language's own word order, marks and negation.
- **Share it.** A timeline where every post is written in its author's letters — the shapes travel with the post, so everyone sees your script exactly as you drew it. Turn any line into a card image to share outside the app.
- **Publish your language.** Give it a page that others can read and, where you allow it, download a chapter of.

## Built with
- Plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript in a Capacitor iOS shell, with the font writer (OpenType) running on the device.
- Supabase for accounts, the timeline and storing every language on the server.
- **RevenueCat SDK** for the Plus and Pro subscriptions (monthly and yearly).

## Monetization
Free: one language, your own a–z and digits, the drawn-letter QWERTY, the timeline.
Plus and Pro: more letters and writing systems, custom keyboard layouts, downloading other people's languages, more languages. A plan decides what you can do — never what you keep: everything made on a paid plan stays after it ends.
